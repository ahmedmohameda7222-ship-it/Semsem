# More intimate requests and everyday love notes — design
**Intent:** Two enrolled adult partners want more private intimate requests plus normal affectionate check-ins, and each must be able to save their own cards and share them.
**UX:** Remove the exact “Little sparks, private moments. Share a feeling — never an obligation.” subtitle. Preserve the existing Intimate Requests section and 3 themes, expand from 15 to 27 cards. Add a separate Daily Love Notes section on Home with at least 15 statements such as “I love you” and “Thinking of you all day.” Both use a preview/edit composer, explicit Send and Cancel. Add “Add your own” card in both sections and let the author delete their custom card; both partners see saved cards.
**Privacy:** Private requests remain server-side is_private=true, generic on lock screen and optional to answer. Daily notes are plain text messages, not titled requests and show ordinary notification content. No automatically sending and no change to existing Love Buttons or legacy RPC.
**Storage:** New RLS-protected shared_cards table with author_id, kind, category, title, message; both enrolled members read, author can create/delete. New locked-down send_private_card RPC validates input, membership, idempotency and handles push outbox. Daily uses existing send_chat RPC.
**Non-goals:** No timelines, auto replies, reminders, media attachments or changes to stored historical messages.
